A short, funny rant about an everyday annoyance, written for the
[Angry Judge: The Short Rant Contest](https://make.fun/competitions/u-riidzhsn-angry-judge-the-short-rant-contest-b9f46046)
on make.fun.

## The rant

> She sent a four-minute voice note. Four minutes. To say "yes." It opens
> with breathing, a long pause, a story about her cat, and somewhere in
> minute three, buried like treasure, the word "yes." Text it. Two letters.
> My thumb is tired from holding the phone like a seashell.

One concrete annoyance — the four-minute voice note that exists only to say
"yes" — told in sharp, economical sentences with a fresh angle: the word
"yes" buried like treasure in minute three, and a thumb tired from holding
the phone like a seashell.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the rant (273 characters, within the 1–400 gate) to
`/output/entry.txt`. Run it with Node 22.18 or later:

```sh
node main.ts
```
